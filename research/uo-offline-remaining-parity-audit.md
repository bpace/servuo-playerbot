# UO Offline remaining parity audit

Checked 2026-09-29 against UO Offline commit [`7f38c7c`](https://github.com/Klein187/uo-offline/tree/7f38c7cd586dc67dc96a4857754e65987351fe2e) and the current ServUO mod. This is a gap audit, not a copy plan. UO Offline is a ModernUO T2A product, so its behavior and engine patches are evidence of intent, not a portable API contract.

## The party and guild baseline is already in the port

The current mod already makes real native `Party` objects for bot-only groups, expires them after fifteen minutes, and lets a nearby real player request a normal invitation and accept it through ServUO. [`PlayerBotParties.cs`](../Scripts/Custom/PlayerBots/PlayerBotParties.cs) uses `Core.UOR` as the era gate and the native Party API. It also has a persistent bot-only native guild. [`PlayerBotGuilds.cs`](../Scripts/Custom/PlayerBots/PlayerBotGuilds.cs)

That is a stronger engine integration than upstream's bot-only system: upstream's `BotParty` is a separate coordinator, while only its player-party adapter uses the native Party API. [BotPartySystem.cs](https://github.com/Klein187/uo-offline/blob/7f38c7cd586dc67dc96a4857754e65987351fe2e/playerbots/source/CustomBots/BotPartySystem.cs#L62-L118) [BotPlayerParty.cs](https://github.com/Klein187/uo-offline/blob/7f38c7cd586dc67dc96a4857754e65987351fe2e/playerbots/source/CustomBots/BotPlayerParty.cs#L41-L97)

The remaining parity slice is autonomous social coordination. The port only forms command-created, short-lived parties with leader-follow movement. It lacks upstream's idle-bank recruitment, muster, dungeon march/crawl, straggler recovery, leadership succession, resurrection aid, convoy and warband kinds. [BotPartySystem.cs](https://github.com/Klein187/uo-offline/blob/7f38c7cd586dc67dc96a4857754e65987351fe2e/playerbots/source/CustomBots/BotPartySystem.cs#L256-L405) [BotPartySystem.cs](https://github.com/Klein187/uo-offline/blob/7f38c7cd586dc67dc96a4857754e65987351fe2e/playerbots/source/CustomBots/BotPartySystem.cs#L699-L892) Player groups likewise need member-aware assist, catch-up, voluntary departure and no-auto-rejoin-after-death behavior, not just a native invite. [PlayerGroupBehavior.cs](https://github.com/Klein187/uo-offline/blob/7f38c7cd586dc67dc96a4857754e65987351fe2e/playerbots/source/CustomBots/Behaviors/PlayerGroupBehavior.cs#L76)

Guild parity is further away. The port has one protected bot guild, but not weighted bot affiliations, a social graph, guild speech, player-initiated guild recruitment, crews or faction affiliation. [BotGuilds.cs](https://github.com/Klein187/uo-offline/blob/7f38c7cd586dc67dc96a4857754e65987351fe2e/playerbots/source/CustomBots/BotGuilds.cs#L26-L152) [BotGuildRecruits.cs](https://github.com/Klein187/uo-offline/blob/7f38c7cd586dc67dc96a4857754e65987351fe2e/playerbots/source/CustomBots/BotGuildRecruits.cs#L90-L123) [BotGuildChat.cs](https://github.com/Klein187/uo-offline/blob/7f38c7cd586dc67dc96a4857754e65987351fe2e/playerbots/source/CustomBots/BotGuildChat.cs#L149-L181)

## Remaining world-simulation systems

The port has a behavior registry, destination visits, graveyard activity, native labor shifts, corpse recovery, banking and a real player-to-hawker item sale. Its hawker is deliberately narrow: generated stock and a single bank-funded purchase, with no shopper role, supply chain, pricing market, bot-to-bot settlement or haggling. [`PlayerBotShop.cs`](../Scripts/Custom/PlayerBots/PlayerBotShop.cs) UO Offline's commerce is a connected economy made of stock, buyer demand, bargaining, banking and real transfers. [BotShop.cs](https://github.com/Klein187/uo-offline/blob/7f38c7cd586dc67dc96a4857754e65987351fe2e/playerbots/source/CustomBots/BotShop.cs#L100-L155) [BotShopDeal.cs](https://github.com/Klein187/uo-offline/blob/7f38c7cd586dc67dc96a4857754e65987351fe2e/playerbots/source/CustomBots/BotShopDeal.cs#L64-L119) [BotBanking.cs](https://github.com/Klein187/uo-offline/blob/7f38c7cd586dc67dc96a4857754e65987351fe2e/playerbots/source/CustomBots/BotBanking.cs#L59-L149)

The port's miner, lumberjack and blacksmith are manual ten-minute test shifts. [`PlayerBotLabor.cs`](../Scripts/Custom/PlayerBots/PlayerBotLabor.cs) Remaining parity is scheduled roles with authored gather locations, resource delivery, tool/restock, craft profiles and products feeding commerce. [GathererBehavior.cs](https://github.com/Klein187/uo-offline/blob/7f38c7cd586dc67dc96a4857754e65987351fe2e/playerbots/source/CustomBots/Behaviors/GathererBehavior.cs#L157) [CrafterBehavior.cs](https://github.com/Klein187/uo-offline/blob/7f38c7cd586dc67dc96a4857754e65987351fe2e/playerbots/source/CustomBots/Behaviors/CrafterBehavior.cs#L2-L16)

Still absent as recurring population behavior are taming and pack animals, treasure hunting, SOS/fishing and sea events, housing, full thief loops, faction wars, bank duels, persistent friendships/gossip, and comprehensive dungeon crawling. The upstream behavior registry is the primary inventory of those actor modes. [BehaviorRegistry.cs](https://github.com/Klein187/uo-offline/blob/7f38c7cd586dc67dc96a4857754e65987351fe2e/playerbots/source/CustomBots/Behaviors/BehaviorRegistry.cs#L14-L93) Faction fights and duels should remain later work because they add notoriety, death and crowd-control consequences beyond ordinary travel. [BotFactionWar.cs](https://github.com/Klein187/uo-offline/blob/7f38c7cd586dc67dc96a4857754e65987351fe2e/playerbots/source/CustomBots/BotFactionWar.cs#L137-L184) [BotDuelSystem.cs](https://github.com/Klein187/uo-offline/blob/7f38c7cd586dc67dc96a4857754e65987351fe2e/playerbots/source/CustomBots/BotDuelSystem.cs#L143-L214)

## Navigation gap and era policy

The port has an authored graph and uses bounded direct paths plus local ServUO `MovementPath` fallback. [`PlayerBotWorldData.cs`](../Scripts/Custom/PlayerBots/PlayerBotWorldData.cs) It does not have upstream's HPA graph, distance fields, path follower, cached destination fields, route editor bridge or its full dungeon floor/teleporter model. [HpaGraph.cs](https://github.com/Klein187/uo-offline/blob/7f38c7cd586dc67dc96a4857754e65987351fe2e/playerbots/source/CustomBots/Nav/HpaGraph.cs) [DistanceField.cs](https://github.com/Klein187/uo-offline/blob/7f38c7cd586dc67dc96a4857754e65987351fe2e/playerbots/source/CustomBots/Nav/DistanceField.cs) [PathFollower.cs](https://github.com/Klein187/uo-offline/blob/7f38c7cd586dc67dc96a4857754e65987351fe2e/playerbots/source/CustomBots/Nav/PathFollower.cs)

Do not port the navigation stack before importing and validating graph data facet by facet. UO Offline's data was captured for map 0/T2A terrain, and its own integration notes require ModernUO patches for door pathing and T2A Recall/Gate rules. [INTEGRATION-NOTES.txt](https://github.com/Klein187/uo-offline/blob/7f38c7cd586dc67dc96a4857754e65987351fe2e/INTEGRATION-NOTES.txt#L142) Candidate waypoints must be checked against the live ServUO map, statics, doors, teleporters and regions before use. The existing facet-validation policy remains correct. [T2A/Felucca import note](t2a-felucca-trammel-import-compatibility.md)

The era boundary must be explicit and capability-based. Native Party creation, invitations and player group behavior require `Core.UOR`; the mod already enforces that, consistent with ServUO's `Core.UOR` definition. [Main.cs](../../../../upstream/ServUO-pub57/Server/Main.cs#L144) On pre-UOR shards, suppress those features rather than emulating native party packets. Guild membership is not inherently a UOR feature, but guild chat needs a separate capability check: upstream needed a T2A-specific shim because its normal guild-speech path was `Core.SE` gated. [BotGuildChat.cs](https://github.com/Klein187/uo-offline/blob/7f38c7cd586dc67dc96a4857754e65987351fe2e/playerbots/source/CustomBots/BotGuildChat.cs#L2-L23) Navigation must gate data and travel methods by available maps, regions and spells, not by the label of an expansion.

## Recommended order

First, extend the existing native Party layer into an autonomous coordinator while retaining real-player opt-in only: bank/tavern LFG, compatible-bot selection, muster, leader-follow/assist, clean death and expiry disband. Second, make player-initiated guild acceptance and bot guild crews reusable atop the existing native guild boundary. Third, add an offline-validated graph import and dungeon traversal slice. Only then make gathering, crafting and commerce one economy loop. This order creates visible social life without fabricating engine mechanics or committing to ModernUO-only navigation.
